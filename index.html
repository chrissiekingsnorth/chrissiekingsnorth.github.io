About Me

Warm, engaging narration with a lifelong love of books

Books have always been a huge part of my life. I'm an avid reader, and when I'm not working, my Kindle is rarely far from my side. I'm particularly drawn to romance and mystery; stories filled with compelling characters, emotional connections, secrets, tension and those irresistible "just one more chapter" moments.

Professionally, I work in a secondary school supporting young people, which has given me a warm, patient and approachable communication style.

I am also co-director of my husband's software company, where I contribute to the creation of tutorial and educational content. This includes voice-over narration for instructional videos, as well as reviewing materials to help ensure they are clear, accessible and easy to follow.

That experience introduced me to voice-over work and made me realise how much I enjoy bringing written material to life through narration. As someone who already spends a great deal of time immersed in books, audiobook narration feels like a very natural next step.

Audiobook narration feels like a natural extension of two things I genuinely love: reading and using my voice to communicate. I particularly enjoy character-driven stories and would love the opportunity to bring romance, romantic mystery and emotionally engaging fiction to life for listeners.

I bring warmth, enthusiasm, careful preparation and a genuine respect for an author's characters and story to every project.
